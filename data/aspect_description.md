# Aspect Descriptions

In this table, we describe each high-level Aspect category in the dataset and show representative fine-grained Aspect-labels that characterize the types of connections captured by that category. 

| Aspect | Description | Example Aspect-labels |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **Generalization / Modification** | The related work extends, modifies, or reformulates ideas from the seed by broadening scope, introducing variants, or developing generalized frameworks or methods. The relationship is primarily theory-expanding. | generalization, generalized, extension, modification, variant, analogue, characterization, extends concept, generalized version |
| **Prove / Cases** | The related work focuses on proving results, verifying claims, or treating specific cases of a theory introduced elsewhere. Emphasis is on formal proof, validation, or case-by-case analysis rather than new abstractions. | proof, proved, theorem proven, provided proof, proved cases, complete proof, claims proved, verify cases |
| **Results** | The related work contributes concrete mathematical results within the same problem area, such as new theorems, conclusions, or derived consequences. The connection is based on shared outcomes rather than scope changes. | result, achieved, obtained, determined, observed, conclusion given, main result, precise result, result implies |
| **Specialization / Restriction** | The related work narrows a broader framework by imposing additional assumptions, focusing on a specific class, structure, or setting, or refining previously introduced methods or questions. | revised, described, investigated, considered, applied, introduced, methods, methodology, approach, problem |
